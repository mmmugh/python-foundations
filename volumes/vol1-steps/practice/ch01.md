## Extra practice: a film club flyer

This page is optional: one more project for unit 1, if you want it. Nothing later needs it.

The Riverside Film Club needs a flyer for Friday's showing. You can already print every line of it: text in quotes, blank lines, and numbers Python works out. You will print it a piece at a time, and at the end you will have the whole flyer, printed by one program.

Here is the flyer:

```
======================================
         RIVERSIDE FILM CLUB
======================================

Feature:  The Quiet Harbor  (1998)
When:     Friday, 7:30 pm
Where:    Room 14

Seats available: 96
Popcorn for 3: 11.25

======================================
```

Two of those numbers are not typed in. The room has 8 rows of 12 seats, and a box of popcorn costs 3.75. Let Python work them out.

### Steps

1. Print the top of the flyer: the line of equals signs, the club's name, and the line again. Copy each line from the flyer, spaces and all.
2. Print the feature line, `Feature:  The Quiet Harbor  (1998)`, with one `print()` and one string. It has two spaces after `Feature:`.
3. Print the same line with two strings and a comma between them: `Feature:` and `The Quiet Harbor  (1998)`. Run it. The comma gives you one space, not two, so this is what comes out: `Feature: The Quiet Harbor  (1998)`.
4. Print the `When:` and `Where:` lines, with a `print()` for each. Copy them from the flyer, so their words line up.
5. Print `Seats available: 96`, with the 96 worked out by Python from 8 rows of 12 seats.
6. Print `Popcorn for 3: 11.25`, with the 11.25 worked out by Python from 3 boxes at 3.75 each.
7. Print the whole flyer with one program, blank lines and all, every line in order. Put a comment at the top that says what it prints.

> Say it: in your own words, what does the program print? Write it as a comment at the top. ([How to start a problem](99-how-to-start-a-problem.html))
> Split it: one comment for each part: the top, the showing, the numbers, the line at the bottom.
> Build it: turn the first comment into code, and run it. Then the next, and the next. You have already printed every piece.

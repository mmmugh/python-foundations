WHY IT WORKS
------------

19. Printing `a` shows `[1, 2, 3, 4]` even though only `b` was changed,
    because `b = a` does not make a second list: it makes a second
    __________________ for the __________________ list.

        a = [1, 2, 3]
        b = a
        b.append(4)
        print(a)

20. `.sort()` changes the list itself and gives back __________________,
    which is why `scores = scores.sort()` throws the list away.
